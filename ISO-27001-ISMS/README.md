# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Implementation
### Sadeem – Simulated Data Center (Colocation & IaaS)

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the practical implementation of an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)** based on **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** within a simulated Egyptian data center environment named **Sadeem**.

The project follows a business-driven, risk-based approach to information security governance, addressing the security and compliance requirements associated with providing Colocation and Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) to local and international organizations.

The objective is to establish a structured ISMS that strengthens information security governance, addresses identified risks, and prepares the organization for an independent certification audit.

## 2. Business Scenario

### 2.1 Organizational Background

Sadeem is an Egypt-based data center service provider offering **Colocation and Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)** to local and international organizations across various industries.

The company provides customers with secure physical hosting facilities, infrastructure resources, and cloud-based services to support their critical business operations.

As Sadeem expanded its customer base and pursued international business opportunities, information security and regulatory compliance became increasingly important factors in maintaining customer trust and competitiveness.

### 2.2 Business Challenge

Over time, several international clients began introducing stricter information security requirements as part of their contractual agreements and vendor selection criteria.

In particular, prospective and existing customers started requiring **ISO/IEC 27001 certification** as a prerequisite for entering into new hosting agreements or continuing their existing business relationships.

Although Sadeem had established operational and technical security practices, it lacked a formally implemented and independently certifiable Information Security Management System (ISMS).

This created several business risks:

- Losing existing customers or failing to renew important contracts.
- Being excluded from international tenders and new business opportunities.
- Facing increased scrutiny during customer security assessments and audits.
- Reduced customer confidence in Sadeem's ability to protect hosted information and infrastructure.

### 2.3 Management Decision

Recognizing that information security compliance had become a strategic business requirement, Sadeem's management decided to initiate an **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS implementation project**.

The primary objective was to establish a structured, risk-based ISMS that would address customer security requirements, strengthen internal governance, and prepare the organization for an independent certification audit.

### 2.4 Expected Business Outcomes

Through this initiative, Sadeem aimed to:

- Establish an ISO/IEC 27001:2022-aligned ISMS.
- Improve its ability to identify, assess, and treat information security risks.
- Strengthen information security governance and accountability.
- Demonstrate a structured approach to protecting customer information and infrastructure.
- Improve readiness for customer audits and independent certification.
- Support customer trust, contractual compliance, and international business growth.

## 3. Project Objectives

The project focused on the following objectives:

- Define the ISMS scope and organizational boundaries.
- Evaluate existing information security practices against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
- Identify and assess information security risks.
- Develop appropriate risk treatment strategies.
- Determine applicable information security controls through the Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- Develop the necessary information security policies and supporting documentation.
- Evaluate ISMS implementation through internal audit and management review.

## 4. Standards & Frameworks

The project considered the following international standards:

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** – Information Security Management Systems requirements.
- **ISO/IEC 27017** – Information security controls for cloud services.
- **ISO/IEC 27018** – Protection of personally identifiable information (PII) in public clouds acting as PII processors.

## 5. Implementation Activities

The project covered the following implementation activities:

### 5.1 ISMS Scope Definition

Establishing the scope and boundaries of the ISMS for Sadeem's simulated data center environment, considering its Colocation and IaaS services.

### 5.2 Gap Assessment

Assessing existing information security practices and documentation against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements to identify compliance gaps and areas requiring improvement.

### 5.3 Risk Assessment

Developing a risk assessment methodology and identifying information security risks relevant to the data center's operations, infrastructure, and customer services.

### 5.4 Risk Treatment

Preparing a risk treatment plan to address identified risks through appropriate treatment options and security controls.

### 5.5 Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Documenting the applicability of relevant information security controls and their implementation status.

### 5.6 Policy Development

Preparing information security policies and supporting ISMS documentation to establish organizational security requirements and responsibilities.

### 5.7 Internal Audit

Conducting an internal audit to evaluate ISMS implementation, assess conformity with established requirements, and identify areas for improvement.

### 5.8 Management Review

Performing a management review to evaluate ISMS performance, review audit results, and support management decisions and continual improvement.
