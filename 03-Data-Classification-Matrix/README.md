# Data Classification Matrix

### Egypt PDPL & ISO/IEC 27001:2022

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Data Classification Matrix that aligns **Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 (PDPL)** with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** information classification requirements.

The matrix categorizes organizational data based on its legal status, sensitivity, and information security impact. It also maps relevant ISO controls, identifies key risks, and highlights potential legal and cybercrime penalty exposure.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Classify data according to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law.
- Assign appropriate information security classification labels.
- Map data classification requirements to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls.
- Identify key risks associated with different data types.
- Highlight relevant legal obligations and potential penalties.
- Support consistent data handling and protection decisions.

## 3. Scope of the Matrix

The matrix covers **96 data items across 14 categories**, including:

- Identity and contact information.
- Online and device identifiers.
- Health, genetic, and biometric data.
- Financial information.
- Religious beliefs, political opinions, and security status.
- Children's data.
- Employment and HR information.
- Customer and behavioural data.
- Location and surveillance data.
- Authentication credentials and technical secrets.
- IT infrastructure and operational information.
- Security information.
- Business and corporate information.

## 4. Classification Methodology

The matrix applies two complementary classification perspectives:

### 4.1 Legal Classification – Egypt PDPL

Data is categorized according to Law No. 151 of 2020 and its Executive Regulations, including:

- Personal Data (Not Sensitive).
- Sensitive Personal Data.
- Data that may contain personal or sensitive information depending on its content.
- Data outside the scope of the Law.

### 4.2 Information Security Classification – ISO/IEC 27001:2022

A three-level organizational classification scheme is used:

| Classification | Description                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Internal       | Limited impact if disclosed; accessible to staff and approved contractors.                  |
| Confidential   | Significant impact if disclosed; access restricted on a need-to-know basis.                 |
| Restricted     | Severe impact or legal consequences; access limited to specifically authorized individuals. |

The classification approach considers the highest sensitivity of the information contained in each data item.

## 5. Standards & Regulatory References

- **Egyptian PDPL – Law No. 151 of 2020**
- **Executive Regulations – Decision No. 816 of 2025**
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** – Annex A Controls 5.12 (Classification of Information), 5.13 (Labelling of Information), and 5.34 (Privacy and Protection of PII).
- **ISO/IEC 27002:2022** – Guidance for information security controls.
- **ISO/IEC 27701:2019** – Privacy control references for controllers and processors.
- **Egyptian Cybercrime Law – Law No. 175 of 2018**

## 6. Key Matrix Components

For each data item, the matrix documents:

- Data category and description.
- Classification under Egyptian PDPL.
- Relevant legal and regulatory provisions.
- Assigned ISO information classification label.
- Applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls.
- Relevant ISO/IEC 27701:2019 privacy references.
- Key information security and privacy risks.
- Potential penalties under PDPL and the Cybercrime Law.
